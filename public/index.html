🎯 Problem Statement

Commuters navigating cities with multiple transport options often face severe uncertainty regarding actual arrival times. Standard timetables ignore rush hours and bottlenecks, while passengers frequently have tight deadlines (e.g. reaching the airport by 6 PM). RouteWise AI predicts actual travel times using machine learning and recommends the optimal route meeting strict deadlines.

⚙️ Key Methodologies

  • GTFS Processing: Ingestion of General Transit Feed Specification datasets for Kochi Buses and Kochi Metro.
  • XGBoost Regression: Predicts segment travel duration using spatial coordinates, sequences, and departure times.
  • Random Forest Pipeline: Preprocessed train logs predicting arrival delays in minutes with one-hot encoding.
  • Multi-Modal Comparison: Direct side-by-side evaluation of bus vs metro journey duration and deadline reachability.
